# User Stories

Format: As a [type of user], I want [goal], so that [reason or benefit].

1. As a student, I want to submit written comments about my lecturer anonymously in English, Malay, or a mix of both, so that I can explain what I found helpful or difficult in my own words without my identity being revealed.
2. As a lecturer, I want to see a summary of student sentiment for each aspect of my teaching, so that I know which parts students praised or criticised and what to improve.
