[Students and lecturers]

## What exactly is going wrong?
[Miscommunication on behalf of the students through the feedback given to the lecturer because of the limitations of a scale-based questionaire]

## Why does it matter?
[limits the improvements that the lecturers are able to make because of the lack of information on what in improve]

## Problem statement (3 to 5 sentences)
[A badly designed feedback system is going to have an effect on those who evaluates and the one who is evaluated, which in this case are between students and lecturers. a scale based feedback has a lot of limitations such as limit the students ability to fully explain the strengths and weaknesses of a lecturer which will lead to miscommunication between what students are trying to say and a lecturer trying to improve his/her teaching approach.this will in turn going to affect the future students who will be learning under a flawed system that has not been identified by the lecturer.]
